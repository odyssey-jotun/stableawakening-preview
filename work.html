The Work

Spiritual psychology, explained.

Honoring what’s sacred alongside our modern understanding of the mind. Nothing to believe, nothing to perform.

What it is

Keep your critical thinking.

Many thoughtful people feel drawn toward spirituality but hesitate because they don’t want to give up their groundedness. You don’t have to.

This work draws from depth psychology and contemplative practice, from thinkers like Carl Jung and Pema Chödrön, and from the enduring wisdom of the Buddha, Christ, and the Sufi poets.

No dogma No required beliefs No judgement
Why it works

Sacred wisdom, modern clarity.

vs

Traditional talk therapy

Spiritual psychology works with non-ordinary states: awakening experiences, archetypal energies, and medicine work that go beyond mainstream psychology.

vs

Most religions

It doesn’t force a worldview or ask you to change your beliefs. It pairs ancient wisdom with our modern understanding of the mind and cosmos.

Why people reach out
  • Going through a major life transition
  • Seeking purpose in an overwhelming world
  • Integrating an awakening or psychedelic experience
  • Feeling stuck despite years of therapy or growth work
  • Wanting someone grounded to walk beside them
What becomes possible
  • Grounded inner peace and more self-trust
  • Relief from long-standing emotional burdens
  • Authentic confidence and a clear “North Star”
  • Stability after intense spiritual experiences
  • Connection to your inner truth, and Oneness
Is this for you?

Two kinds of seekers.

Either way, the work is about awakening to your true Self and living it every day.

Thoughtful beginners

Curious about meditation or spiritual practice but unsure where to start. Secular, agnostic, or questioning, yet sensing there’s more.

Experienced seekers

Years of books, retreats, maybe plant medicine, yet some patterns still feel unresolved, or the “Aha!” hasn’t clicked.

FAQ

Common questions.

Will you try to convince me of anything?

No.

Do I need to be religious, or believe in spirituality?

No. There’s nothing you’re required to believe.

Can I work with you if I’m atheist or agnostic?

Yes. My own spiritual journey was rooted in a naturalistic worldview.

Do you work with psychedelic experiences?

Yes. I don’t offer or push psychedelics, but I do offer preparation and integration support.

Is this therapy?

No. There’s overlap, but spiritual guidance isn’t a replacement for mental health therapy.

Do you work with people outside New Orleans?

Yes. Clients join from Austin, Seattle, Phoenix, and beyond the U.S.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. No fee, no pressure, no obligation.

Getting started

You don’t have to figure it out alone.

Not a teacher to direct you, but someone to walk beside you, in a space where being human is allowed.
